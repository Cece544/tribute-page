His story

At 58, Patrick Musila carries a lifetime of wisdom, strength and quiet love.

He is the kind of man who leads by example: he works hard, keeps his word and always puts his family first. Through every season of life, he has been our provider, protector and biggest supporter.

He gave his time, his energy and his best years so that we could have a good home, an education and a bright future.
I am Cece, and I am proud to call him my dad.

His many roles

A great dad wears many hats. Here are the ones he wears best:

  • ProviderWorks tirelessly so our needs are met and our dreams stay alive.
  • ProtectorMakes us feel safe, wherever we are.
  • TeacherGuides us with patience, advice and good example.
  • EncouragerBelieves in us, even when we doubt ourselves.
  • FriendListens, laughs with us and stands by us.

Together these roles make him the GOAT of dads.
